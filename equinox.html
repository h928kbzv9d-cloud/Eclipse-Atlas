Equinox times are shown in UTC because the event is a global instant. Equilux is location-specific: it uses the selected latitude and longitude, standard sunrise/sunset refraction and the Sun’s apparent upper-limb correction. Local civil time can differ with your time zone and daylight-saving rules.

This is an astronomical planning estimate, not an observing or legal timekeeping service.
